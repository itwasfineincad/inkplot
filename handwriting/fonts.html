Your fonts

    Open a font to see it or redraw a letter. To write with it, pick it on the Write page under Look.

    Three ways to make one

    1. Draw it here

    Draw each letter in its box. The pen follows exactly what you draw.

    2. Scan your writing

    Print the template at 100% and fill in each box with a dark pen. Scan it, or photograph the page flat with all four black squares in view.

    Download the template

    3. Import a font file

    A .ttf or .otf font is traced to single pen lines. Single-line .svg fonts are used as they are.
