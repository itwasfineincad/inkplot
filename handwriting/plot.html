      How printing works
      1. Home from the printer screen with the holder off, then clip it on.
      2. Print the jobs in order, ticking each one off. The highlighted job says what to do with the paper first.
      3. Part 2 of a sheet is printed with the same sheet turned round (top edge at the front), so the pen can reach the bottom of the page.
      4. New drawing? Save the Pen up version too and watch it first.
      5. When you're done, take the holder off before homing again.

      Calibrate the pen

      The pen sits beside the nozzle, so the printer has to know how far away. Do this once, and again whenever you re-clip the holder or move the pen in it. Files go to the folder chosen under Save to.

      1Pen

      mm
      mm

      To set the pen to that length: home with the holder off, clip it on with the pen pulled up, and print the pen-height file. The paper comes up to exactly that far below the nozzle and waits 4 minutes while you slide the pen down until it just touches, then tighten it.

      2Draw a calibration cross

      Home with the holder off, clip it on, put a sheet in the back-left corner with the magnets. The nozzle goes to X128 Y180 and the pen draws a cross wherever it really is. Not sure it will clear? Print the pen-up version first.

      Write a page

      Puts text onto your paper's ruled lines, sheet after sheet. The pen can't reach the top or bottom of a sheet in one go, so lines near the bottom are written after you turn the sheet round. Ruled lines it can't reach at all are left empty.

      These come from Write on lined paper in the Paper card; change them there.
